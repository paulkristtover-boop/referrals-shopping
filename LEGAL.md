# Legal & compliance structure (Referrals Shopping)

## Documents (in-app)
- `/legal` — hub
- `/terms` — Terms of Service
- `/privacy` — Privacy Policy
- `/refunds` — Refund policy
- `/guide`, `/faq`, `/support`

## Product rules encoded
- 18+ confirmation on register
- Terms + Privacy acceptance
- Marketplace model (intermediary, not offer issuer)
- ₦ transparent pricing
- Honest delivery language (no guaranteed ROI on third-party investments)
- Tax invoice support (see TAX.md)
- Acceptable use: no fraud / fake proofs

## Competition positioning
Publish clear package prices and the Promote+Earn model. Do not invent competitor stats or fake social proof.

## Operator obligations (outside the app)
- Business registration and tax registration where required
- Payment provider compliance (Flutterwave KYC)
- Content moderation of promoted links
- Local advertising and gambling laws for offers you allow

Not legal advice.
